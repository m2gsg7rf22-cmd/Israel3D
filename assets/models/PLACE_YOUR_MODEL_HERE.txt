Drop your player model here as: player.glb

characterRig.js loads it automatically from ./assets/models/player.glb on
next page load. A single self-contained .glb (glTF Binary) is required --
if you only have an .fbx, re-export it as .glb (Blender: File > Export >
glTF 2.0, format "glTF Binary (.glb)"; Mixamo: download as FBX, then run it
through Blender once to re-export as .glb).

If the .glb includes animation clips named containing "idle", "walk", or
"run"/"sprint" (case-insensitive), they'll be crossfaded automatically based
on movement speed. If it has no matching clips (common for a static/posed
scan like a Renderpeople export), the model will still move and rotate with
the player but won't animate its limbs.
